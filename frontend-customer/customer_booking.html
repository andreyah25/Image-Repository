Down Payment & Cancellation Policy

  • Non-Refundable: All down payments are strictly non-refundable once payment verification is complete.
  • Exact Amount & Verification: Receipts must match the exact required down payment. Submitting fake, altered, or inaccurate proofs will result in booking cancellation, gallery access restriction, and blacklisting.
  • Slot Reservation: Your selected date and time slot will only be officially reserved once proof of down payment is uploaded and confirmed.
  • Cancellation & No-Show: Failure to show up or cancelling less than 24 hours before your shoot will result in the immediate forfeiture of your down payment.
  • Remaining Balance: The remaining balance of your total package fee must be settled in full on or before the day of the photo session.
