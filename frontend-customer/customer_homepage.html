NEED TO KNOW?

Frequently asked questions

Here are some quick answers about booking, sessions, and accessing your photos.

Book a Session
How do I book a session?

Click the Book a Session button and complete the booking form. You can choose your preferred package, date, and available time.

Is the studio private?

Yes. Captured is designed to provide a comfortable and private self-photography experience.

How can I access my photos?

Use the Access My Photos option on the homepage and submit your gallery access request using your booking information.

Can I bring friends or family?

Yes. Group and family-oriented session options are available depending on the package you select.
