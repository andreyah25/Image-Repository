Gallery Access Regulations

Please read and understand the following regulations before requesting access to your photo gallery.

  1. Gallery access is only available to customers with a completed booking.
  2. The email address you provide must match the email address used for your booking.
  3. Your first approved gallery access is free and is valid for 24 hours from the time access is granted.
  4. After the first 24-hour free access expires, a second gallery access request may be submitted.
  5. The second gallery access requires a ₱100 payment. Once payment is confirmed, the second access is valid for 3 days.
  6. After the second access expires, a third and final gallery access request may be submitted. The third access also requires a ₱100 payment and is valid for 3 days after payment confirmation.
  7. After the third gallery access expires, no additional gallery access requests will be available for the booking.
  8. Gallery access requests are subject to administrator approval.
  9. Please do not share your gallery link with other people.
  10. Photos are provided for personal use unless otherwise agreed with the studio.
